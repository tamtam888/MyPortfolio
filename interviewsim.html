InterviewSim

A bilingual interview practice experience designed to help candidates prepare with realistic questions, selectable interviewers, actionable feedback and clear progress summaries.

The product experience

From choosing a role and interviewer to answering questions and reviewing feedback, the interface is structured as a focused, guided practice journey.

01 · Personalized setup

Choose role, experience level, difficulty and interviewer.

02 · Guided practice

Focus on one question at a time with typed or supported voice responses.

03 · Actionable feedback

Review answer-level scoring, strengths and suggestions for improvement.

04 · Session summary

See an overall score, next steps and individual answer reviews.

Design and implementation

The experience prioritizes a clear visual hierarchy, responsive lavender-and-white UI, accessible interaction patterns and bilingual layouts. Interviewer personas help distinguish coaching styles, while the evaluation experience communicates concrete improvement opportunities.

AI evaluation is optional. The application also supports local feedback when the AI service is unavailable. Example screenshots are illustrative of the product interface.
