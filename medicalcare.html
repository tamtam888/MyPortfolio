MedicalCare

Beta Pilot

A beta HealthTech platform delivered to real pilot users, including physiotherapists and hydrotherapists, to validate patient management, treatment workflows, scheduling, AI-assisted documentation, and video-based progress tracking.

Built for therapists and small clinical teams

A short Remotion product walkthrough showing the MedicalCare workflow, core modules, AI-assisted summaries, and Motion Review as a built-in video progress tracking module.

The Problem

Therapists work with a lot of information - patient histories, appointment schedules, session notes, treatment plans, and progress records. In many workflows, this information is spread across separate tools, and moving between them is slow and repetitive.

  • Repetitive administrative work

    Therapists spend significant time on manual computer tasks between and during sessions - time that adds up across a full day of appointments.

  • Scattered clinical information

    Patient records, appointment history, session notes, and progress tracking often live in separate places, making it harder to get a full picture quickly.

  • Workflow friction

    Time spent navigating systems creates friction in the therapist's daily workflow. And for therapists running independent or small-group practices, that time has a real cost.

  • Manual documentation overhead

    Writing up session notes and summaries after each appointment takes time that could be reduced with better tooling and structured workflows.

The Solution

MedicalCare centralizes the key parts of a therapist's administrative workflow into one organized platform. Patient records, appointment context, treatment workflows, AI-assisted session summaries, and video progress tracking are all accessible from a single dashboard.

◈
Everything in one place

A single dashboard brings together the tools therapists use most, so there is less switching between systems during a session.

◈
Button-driven actions

Navigation is designed around clear, button-based actions - common tasks are reachable in fewer steps and the interface reflects how therapists actually work.

◈
Structured workflows

The product supports a more organized and efficient daily routine for therapists, reducing the overhead that comes with manual record-keeping and documentation.

Core Modules

MedicalCare is organized around the workflows therapists use most. Each module is accessible from the main dashboard through clear, direct actions.

Patient Records

Therapists can view and manage structured patient profiles, including session history and relevant clinical notes. Records are organized for quick access, not buried in nested menus.

Appointments

A clear view of upcoming and past appointments helps therapists prepare and follow up without switching between tools. Appointment context is surfaced alongside the relevant patient record.

AI-Assisted Treatment Summaries

Therapists can generate and refine session summaries using built-in AI assistance. The feature is designed to reduce the time spent on documentation after sessions, not to replace clinical writing.

Video Progress Tracking - Motion Review

Motion Review is a module inside MedicalCare that allows therapists to record, review, and annotate video for tracking physical or movement-based progress over time. It is part of the platform, not a separate product.

Therapist Workflow Dashboard

The dashboard gives therapists a starting point for their session workflows. From here they can navigate to any module, see their current patient list, and move between tasks with minimal friction.

How It Works

A typical session workflow in MedicalCare looks like this:

  1. 1
    Open the dashboard and select a patient

    The session starts from the dashboard - choose a patient from the current session list to bring up their full context.

  2. 2
    Review the patient record

    Previous notes, appointment history, and relevant clinical context are all in one view - no switching between tabs or systems.

  3. 3
    Begin the intake or treatment workflow

    Use the button-based action panel to move through the session workflow - structured steps replace scattered manual tasks.

  4. 4
    Generate or refine session notes with AI assistance

    After the session, use the AI summary tool to draft or refine session documentation - reducing the time spent on post-session writing.

  5. 5
    Open Motion Review if tracking movement progress

    When physical or movement-based progress tracking is relevant, the Motion Review module is accessible directly from within the patient's workflow.

  6. 6
    Return to the dashboard

    The session ends with an organized record - ready for the next appointment without loose ends.
